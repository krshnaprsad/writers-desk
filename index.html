What this can and cannot tell you

The counts are exact. The marks are only my opinion of tired phrases, filler and padded words, from lists you can read and change in data/phrases.js. Plenty of good writing uses them on purpose. It does not tell you whether a person or an AI wrote something. It works on English text and runs completely in your browser.
